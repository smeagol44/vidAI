# Featured Starters and the Specimen Bank

VIDA now distinguishes **nine featured starting genotypes** from the **55
historical founder designs**. The Bank's collection control selects
**Featured**, **Legacy**, **My saved specimens** or **All**; the existing text
search, diet/family filters, previews and Spawn buttons operate inside the
chosen collection. All original FND genomes remain usable.

The nine come from **nine different original anatomical families**, not nine
recolored versions of one swimmer. Each new CUR identity has its own part and
organ IDs, while preserving the donor's valid structural connections,
physical mouth position, storage and egg economics. Three featured designs
add selected motor alleles from the bounded swimming search; the remaining six
retain their original motor genes. Mutations can still change anatomy, organs, synapses, receptors
and inherited biological traits.

## What changes genetically?

Each eye on a featured founder begins with inherited RGB
`spectralSensitivity` values in the normal 0–1 range. The two featured
carnivores have especially low or zero **green** gain. This changes what the
physical retinal rays can detect, not whether a stimulus is called a
nutrient, prey or predator. Mutations can decrease, increase or restore the
channels, including a previously absent green channel. Sensor upkeep
continues to depend on active receptors.

Some designs begin with low inherited motor and retinal plasticity rates.
These are the existing energy-paid waking-learning mechanisms, not
preprogrammed foraging or learned survival. A creature can evolve away from
these starting values. The original legacy blueprints do **not** acquire
the new alleles when loaded.

## Swimming calibration

New Tidewheel Browser (CUR-003), Hammerhead Pursuer (CUR-008) and Branchstalker
(CUR-009) genomes receive selected frequency/amplitude/phase alleles. Their
anatomy, neural matrices, receptor and learning genes, starting reserve and
lifecycle economics are preserved. All selected alleles remain mutable through
normal offspring mutation. Saved animals and stored genomes are never rewritten.

The offline search retains each anatomical family separately and screens
sustained COM progress and progress per reserve joule across longer swims,
headings and viscosities. It rejects caps, drift, exhausted reserves and
regressions in late motion or original direction. Six featured designs were
left unchanged because their candidate did not pass every screen. This measures
swimming, not successful hunting, mouth-first travel or sustainable ecology.
See [Movement and Motor Control](Motor-Control) for the current protocol.

## Starting a world

A normal new world still creates the configured founder population (18 by
default) from **at most five randomly selected featured designs**. The default
living-population cap is **30**, and the plant-food target is **275**. It
does not seed every one of the nine. The Bank's optional **Featured
Showcase** resets the world with one of each nine. Older saved worlds
keep their actual stored genomes instead of being converted to CUR IDs.

The initial seed is randomly chosen when the app opens. The settings field
shows that seed and remains editable; resets reuse the displayed seed for
reproducibility. Imported worlds retain their stored settings and random state.

## Selecting a creature

Clicking a creature opens its general-status window. Use **Inspect** there
(or the explicit toolbar/keyboard inspector control) to open **Recurrent
Brain & Diagnostics**. Each opening starts the display-only pruning threshold
at **0.5**; the slider can reveal weaker connections when needed. Pruning
changes only what the graph draws, never the organism's neural connections.

## Evidence and limitations

Focused native-physics checks cover basic displacement, valid joints and
organ anchors, real food-mouth permissions, nonnegative reserves and
the conserved energy ledger. These checks are **not** proof that all
nine independently forage, hunt live prey, escape predators, reproduce
without assistance or persist for generations. No artificial energy
subsidies, food-seeking scripts, forced swimming, or neural fitness scores
were introduced. Establishing such ecological outcomes is the next
development priority.
